V2Ray for Huawei

A self-hosted V2Ray / Xray tunnel that runs directly on a Huawei LTE router, with a web panel to control it from any phone or laptop on your network.

Panel 1.0Huawei B310s-925Xray-core (V2Ray compatible)Trojan · VLESS · Shadowsocks

What it does

  • Whole-LAN tunnelEvery device on the router's WiFi and LAN goes through the encrypted tunnel, with nothing to install on them.
  • UDP controlTunnel, block or pass UDP: QUIC, calls and WebRTC leak protection.
  • Fast on small hardwareTLS 1.3 with ChaCha20, about twice as fast as AES on this router's CPU.
  • Self-healingStarts at boot, and a watchdog restarts the tunnel and firewall rules if anything drops.
  • Router-account loginSign in with the router's own admin account; the password is hashed in your browser.
